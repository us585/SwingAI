A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A little
clarity.

Where does Swing run?+

Swing’s server runs on your Windows PC and connects to a compatible AI model server you configure. This website introduces Swing; the AI chat runs in the Swing app.

Can I use it on my phone or iPad?+

Yes. Keep your PC and Swing server running, connect to the same trusted Wi-Fi, and open the network address shown when the server starts. Sign in to access your account’s saved chats.

Does Agent mode act on its own?+

No. It proposes one supported action at a time and asks for approval. File tools are limited to your Agent workspace. Desktop control requires the original account, and you review screenshots before sharing them with your configured model server.

Where are my chats saved?+

Saved chats live under your account on your PC’s Swing server. They can be accessed from other signed-in devices on your network. Appearance settings and training-example collections remain stored in your browser.

What do I need to get started?+

Download Swing for Windows and keep your Swing server running on its PC. The app uses Microsoft Edge. For screenshot-based PC clicks, choose a vision-capable model.
